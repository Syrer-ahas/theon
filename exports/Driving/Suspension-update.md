Driving uses independently suspended cylinder contacts with spring and prismatic constraints. Own-body collision is excluded for suspension wheels. Tire meshes and rims keep their original geometry.

Ground contact gates traction and orientation assistance. There is no hover force or step-lift force. Airborne cars fall under gravity. Controls remain WASD/arrows, Space brake, E exit, and the existing Drive button; the added HUD, touch buttons, and custom player driving animation were removed.

Verified in Studio: idle settlement, ramp climbing and braking, 0.25-stud curb crossing, airborne fall with zero traction and disabled orientation assistance, landing settlement, and exit restoration. Larger sharp obstacles still depend on tire size and the player's bumper clearance. Inventory and stored build records were not modified by verification.
